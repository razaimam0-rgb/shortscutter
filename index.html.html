Got Questions?
Frequently Asked Questions
Is ShortsCutter really free?▼
Yes, 100% free. ShortsCutter runs entirely on your own computer with no subscriptions, no credits, and no hidden costs — ever. A Pro plan is available for power users who need more clips, higher quality, and advanced features.
Do I need to upload my videos anywhere?▼
No. Everything runs locally on your PC. Your video files are never uploaded to any server. All processing — cutting, subtitles, face tracking — happens right on your computer.
What is the difference between Free and Pro?▼
Free users get 3 clips per video, 720p quality, 2 caption styles, and English subtitles only. Pro users get up to 15 clips, 4K quality, all 7 caption styles, 16 subtitle languages, face tracking, and no watermark.
How do I activate Pro after paying?▼
After payment via Razorpay, you'll receive a license key via email (SC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Enter this key in the tool's "Upgrade Pro" section to instantly unlock all Pro features.
Which operating systems are supported?▼
ShortsCutter works on Windows 10/11 and macOS. Linux support is available for advanced users. Python 3.11 or higher is required.
Can I use my own videos instead of YouTube?▼
Yes! Switch to "Local Video" mode and upload any MP4, MOV, MKV, or AVI file directly from your computer. All the same AI features work on your own videos.
